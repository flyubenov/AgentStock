# Agent Stock — Comprehensive Legal Disclaimers & Terms of Use Framework

## 1. Core Operating Principles for Liability Mitigation

To protect yourself and your business entity from lawsuits, regulatory fines, or consumer disputes regarding the future performance of financial assets, you must strictly establish a **"No Investment Advice / Information Only"** operating framework. Because your system uses deterministic quantitative financial math to compute values, you must clearly frame the product as a mathematical processing tool rather than an active human or robotic advisor.

The structural rules to prevent liability rest on four pillars:
1. **Algorithmic Transparency:** Framing all metrics as objective calculations built directly on public data feeds rather than personal market predictions or subjective choices.
2. **Deterministic Mechanics:** Stating plainly that your software runs standard financial equations (e.g., DCF, Gordon Growth, EV/Sales) using user-adjustable assumptions, making it a mechanical calculator.
3. **Fiduciary Disavowal:** Explicitly denying any fiduciary relationship or personal financial advisory service between you and the user.
4. **The Principle of Past Performance:** Reinforcing the legal standard that historical accounting data, current balances, and calculated trends are trailing indicators that do not guarantee future stock market returns.
