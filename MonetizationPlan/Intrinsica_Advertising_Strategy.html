Intrinsica — Advertising & Popularization Strategy

Exact order, stages, platforms, setup, priorities, and what to do / avoid.

Core principle: Don't advertise Intrinsica first. Advertise the insight it produces.

A person is much more likely to click “Is NVDA overvalued?” than “Try my new stock-analysis SaaS.”

6. Stage 4 — Reddit

Do not post:

12. What NOT to do

  • Don't advertise the technology. Avoid leading with “AI-powered”, “revolutionary”, “next-generation”, etc.
  • Don't advertise the company. Lead with an investor problem or question.
  • Don't optimize for followers. Paid-plan intent matters more.
  • Don't optimize for CTR alone. Curiosity clicks can have little commercial value.
  • Don't send every ad to the homepage. Match intent to a relevant stock analysis.
  • Don't launch ten channels simultaneously. Start with Google + X, then Reddit.
  • Don't spend heavily before validating the message.
  • Don't build complicated marketing infrastructure first. For the smoke test, landing page + analytics + fake pricing/checkout + ads + measurement are enough.
