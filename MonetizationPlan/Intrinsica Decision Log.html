Handoff notes

Six things the events sheet will not tell whoever analyses it.

  1. There is no server-side dedupe. routers/events.py appends one row per event. Spec §9's "de-duplicate server-side by visitor_id" is an instruction about how to count, not something the endpoint does.
  2. The primary metric is unique visitors reaching payment_button_clicked ÷ unique visitors — not event counts. Count distinct visitor_id.
  3. free_plan_clicked fires from two funnel stages. Group by (visitor_id, event, source): source: 'pricing' is the CTA click, source: 'checkout' is the confirm. Grouping without source collapses them and loses free-path drop-off.
  4. source carries different vocabularies on different events. analysis_started and analysis_completed are 'sample' | 'typed' (sample = marketing auto-run, not visitor work); free_plan_clicked is 'pricing' | 'checkout'. Never pivot on source alone.
  5. Abandonment = checkout_started − payment_button_clicked, computed over plan != 'Free' only. The spec's formula is wrong computed globally: Free routes through the same screen and never fires the paid event, so every completed Free journey would otherwise count as an abandonment.
  6. free_plan_clicked is excluded from paid-intent conversion, by spec.
