**Root cause:** LITE is a cyclical/AI-optical name coming out of a deep earnings trough (rev +90% YoY, forward EPS $18.35 vs trailing $5.66 = 3.24×). The capex-distorted **negative-FCF reroute** fired (statement FCF −$104.7M via `real_fcf`, EBITDA+OCF positive → 0.85 EV/EBITDA / 0.15 P/E). That reroute's premise (trust EV/EBITDA, distrust EPS — built for IREN's bitcoin-gain-inflated EPS) is **inverted** for a trough name: the EV/EBITDA leg projected `ev_ebitda_hist_base` = **$106.8M** (a trough statement EBITDA — 13.4× below forward net income of $1.43B; net income can't exceed EBITDA, the smoking gun) at the trough-measured 23× median, giving $46.71 at 0.85 weight. The forward-P/E leg ($623.92, off forward EPS) is the trustworthy anchor but was down-weighted to 0.15. Market $693, analyst consensus Buy / $829.73 target (+20%) — the model was the outlier.

**Fix (Design D — invert the reroute split on a severe trough).** In `engine.evaluate` both capex-reroute branches now flip when a severe forward-EPS trough is present: neg-FCF `0.85/0.15 → 0.15/0.85`, pos-FCF `0.70/0.30 → 0.30/0.70`. **Detector reuses `models.rebased_dcf_base(fin) is not None`, GATED on `stock_type in FORWARD_TIERS`** — no new constant, inherits ALL rebased_dcf_base's guards (severe `forward_eps ≥ TROUGH_REBASE_RATIO(2.5)×eps_ttm` ratio, impossible-net-margin rejection, only-help, positive-data). This mirrors the existing philosophy where `rebased_dcf_base` caps the rebased DCF *at* the forward-P/E leg — forward P/E is the trusted trough anchor. Rejected alternatives: (E) rebase the EV/EBITDA base to forward EBITDA — the 23× multiple was *measured on* the trough base, so keeping it over-values ($841, above target); (F) skip the reroute and let normal GROWTH weights + rebased DCF carry — reintroduces the volatile ev_sales leg the reroute exists to avoid. 3 tests added, **397 pass** (was 394).

**Inline Opus review (not the paid ultrareview) — 0 Critical, 1 Important (fixed), 2 Minor (fixed).** IMPORTANT catch: the reroute's P/E leg is valued FORWARD only for FORWARD_TIERS (`calc_pe(forward=is_forward_tier)`); non-forward tiers with DCF weight — **CYCLICAL (0.40), ASSET_HEAVY (0.30), DIVIDEND (0.25)** — can also reach the negative-FCF reroute, and a CYCLICAL Energy trough (low revenue growth dodges the GROWTH rule, EPS recovering ≥2.5×, negative FCF) would have inverted onto a TRAILING (trough-depressed) P/E — NOT the forward anchor the inversion assumes. Fixed by gating `trough_rebase` on `stock_type in FORWARD_TIERS` (added `test_evaluate_reroute_trough_inversion_only_on_forward_tiers`). Minors: pinned the positive-FCF non-trough 0.70 default in an existing control; trimmed the "SNPS/LITE" comment to "LITE" (SNPS never reaches this reroute — healthy FCF, takes the DCF-rebase path). LESSON: when a reroute forces a leg's weight, check whether that leg's BASIS (forward vs trailing P/E) actually holds for every tier that can reach it.
