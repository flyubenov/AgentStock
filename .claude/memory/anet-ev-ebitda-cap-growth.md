ANET quality 9.5 is SOUND (elite: 27% rev CAGR, 25% ROIC, net cash, 63% GM). Its FV of $104 (-38% vs $168) was NOT judgment but three stacked artifacts. DONE + MERGED to master (no-ff `589a7c7`, fix @`e486d06`; branch `fix/ev-ebitda-cap-and-growth-scenarios`), 307 tests (5 added).

**Root causes + fixes (all `backend/valuation/`):**
1. **EV/EBITDA cap**: flat `EV_EBITDA_CAP=20` clamped the pipeline's OWN reconstructed 27.5x historical median → leg $76 not $104. Fixed with `_ev_ebitda_ceiling(growth, durable, mega)`: ramps 20x→30x (G_LO 0.10 → G_HI 0.30) but ONLY for a durable hist median (`hist_multiple is not None`); a spot trailing multiple still caps at 20x (could be a one-quarter EBITDA dip). Same latent bug the BWXT/KLAC/NFLX notes flagged ("EV_EBITDA_CAP 20 makes hist multiples moot").
2. **Mega-cap ceiling**: >=`MEGA_CAP_FLOOR` ($1T) ramps to `EV_EBITDA_CAP_CEIL_MEGA=25`, not 30 — a sustained mega-cap premium multiple is a stronger claim (size base-rate drag, mirrors `_fade_hold_years`). Keyed off `fin['market_cap']`. NVDA at 30x→+6% (too generous), 25x→-2.5% (fair), 20x→-11% (= original hard cap, refuses any franchise premium). User chose 25x.
3. **Scenario collapse**: `optimistic = min(base+0.05, cap)` collapsed to realistic whenever the cap bound → 3-way avg biased strictly BELOW realistic (only pessimistic had room). Fixed: `GROWTH_OPT_HEADROOM=0.05` lifts optimistic to `cap+0.05`, BUT only when `raw <= cap+headroom` (corroborated grower ANET). A hyper-grower pinned far below raw by the ceiling (IREN 167%, NBIS 684%) keeps optimistic AT cap — the noise backstop must hold in the bull case too, or the suppressed noise leaks back through the optimistic leg.
4. **Growth cap**: `GROWTH_CAP_SLOPE 0.125→0.60`, ceiling LEFT at 0.25 (NOT raised — raising it to 0.30 lifted IREN's 167%-pinned realistic 0.25→0.30, the regression signal). Steeper slope lets a ~28% corroborated grower REACH the existing 0.25 ceiling (ANET raw=25% earnings growth → realistic 0.25 not 0.21). EARLY_GROWTH keeps its own shallow `EG_CAP_SLOPE=0.125` via `_growth_cap(g, ceil, slope)` — so NBIS/TEM DON'T move.
